# Grok Bot Sharing Contest — entry checklist

**The contest runs 15–29 September 2026. Winners are picked mostly by how many people clone the bot template, then by creativity, originality, and brand fit.**

## 1. Publish the bot template

1. Open the Pixel Office bot in Grok Bot.
2. Select **Share as Template** in bot settings.
3. Review the items included in the template. Remove any private memory, key, or URL.
4. Publish the template as public.
5. Copy the template link (a public `x.ai/bot/…` URL).
6. Put the link in `README.md` in place of `REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_TEMPLATE_LINK`.

## 2. Make the site ready (done)

- Live URL: <https://abdelrahmanmagdii.github.io/pixel-office/>
- Open Graph card: `og-image.png` is live. X shows the card when you paste the URL.
- Demo controls: Play tour, Randomize busy states, per-agent Idle / Working / Waiting, CoS Desk↔Patrol toggle.

## 3. Post the entry

1. Quote @grok's contest post. Do not create a new post.
2. Say what the bot does in one line.
3. Paste the template link.
4. Make sure the account is public.
5. Follow @grok and @bot from a public account.

## 4. Drive clones

- Attach a 15-second screen recording of the office to the quote post. Video drives reach.
- Put the template link in the README, in this file, and in the tweet.
- Make the repository a GitHub template: Settings → General → **Template repository**. Then anyone can select **Use this template** and get their own floor.

## 5. Rules to respect

- Entrants must be US residents, 18 or older.
- Not available in Colorado, Maryland, Nebraska, and North Dakota.
- Winners are announced around October 21.
